# Customer Notification SOP

## When to Notify Customers

Customer notification should be considered when a production incident
has a material customer impact.

## Notification Process

1. Confirm the incident impact.
2. Identify affected customers or customer segments.
3. Prepare a concise description of the issue.
4. Describe the expected customer impact.
5. Provide the current mitigation or workaround when available.
6. Obtain the required approval.
7. Send the notification through the approved communication channel.

## Important Rule

Do not communicate unverified technical details to customers.

Customer-facing communication must be based on confirmed information.

## Incident Resolution

After the incident is resolved, provide an appropriate resolution
update when customer notification was previously issued.
